# Risk Disclaimer

## What India OpenRisk Is

India OpenRisk is a research and educational tool that estimates the probability of an elevated- or high-risk opening for the Nifty 50 index, based on overnight U.S. market data and selected U.S. news. It is built to demonstrate a risk-analysis methodology, not to generate trading signals.

## What India OpenRisk Is Not

- **Not investment advice.** Nothing produced by this system is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security, index, or derivative.
- **Not a price forecast.** The system outputs a probabilistic risk *regime* (normal / elevated / high), never a predicted price, return, or index level.
- **Not a causal claim.** When the system cites overnight news alongside a risk score, it is reporting a correlation-based association, not asserting that the news caused any market movement.
- **Not guaranteed to be accurate.** All outputs are probabilistic estimates from a statistical/ML model trained on historical data. Historical relationships between U.S. overnight signals and Nifty 50 behavior may weaken, disappear, or reverse without warning.

## Model Limitations

- The model is trained and validated on historical data and may not generalize to unprecedented market conditions (e.g., unprecedented geopolitical shocks, structural breaks, regime changes).
- Backtested performance does not guarantee future performance.
- Data-quality issues (missing, late, or stale inputs) can degrade output reliability; the system flags these where detected, but cannot guarantee upstream data providers are error-free.
- The model considers only U.S.-market inputs (Version 1 scope) and does not account for domestic Indian catalysts, other global markets, or intraday developments after the report is generated.

## Intended Use

This tool is intended for educational and portfolio-demonstration purposes, to illustrate a risk-analysis workflow (data validation, baseline benchmarking, model validation, evidence-grounded AI reporting). It is not intended for use in live trading or investment decision-making without independent professional judgment.
